## "What this is, and is not": the paragraph, with one sentence appended

```markdown
**What this is, and is not.** A LoRA adapter for one narrow, synthetic
tool-calling task (four tools, 158 generated training examples, 29 held-out
prompts), published to show the training mechanism and its reproducibility.
It is not a general tool-use upgrade for Qwen3-4B, and the training recipe
(316 steps at lr 1e-4 on 158 examples) is memorization-grade, right for a
format habit and wrong for a knowledge task. Measured off its task, it does
not help: on 274 hand-written prompts unlike its training, over 23 tools with
overlapping names, the base makes more exact calls through the chat API than
the adapter (211 to 200), and the adapter helps only where no tool fits
([the shifted set](https://github.com/Joakimpalm-Zen/xyntetik-runner/blob/main/evals/tooluse-shifted/README.md#set-v2-2026-09-24-the-redesign-and-can-the-instrument-fail)).
```
