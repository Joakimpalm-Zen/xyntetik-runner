Mercury is a small, rocky planet with extreme temperature swings and almost no atmosphere.
Venus is a hot, thick-atmosphere world with a crushing surface pressure and retrograde rotation.
Earth is the only known world with life, liquid surface water, and a breathable oxygen-rich atmosphere.
Mars is a cold, reddish desert world with polar caps, dust storms, and the tallest volcano in the Solar System.
Jupiter is the largest planet, a gas giant famous for its Great Red Spot and numerous moons.
Saturn is a gas giant distinguished by its extensive ring system and low average density.
Uranus is an ice giant with a strongly tilted axis and a pale blue-green appearance.
Neptune is a deep-blue ice giant with powerful winds and active storms.
