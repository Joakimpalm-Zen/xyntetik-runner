Mercury is the smallest and innermost planet, with a heavily cratered surface and extreme temperature variations due to its thin atmosphere.  
Venus is the second planet from the Sun, known for its thick, toxic atmosphere and extreme greenhouse effect causing surface temperatures hot enough to melt lead.  
Earth is the third planet from the Sun, the only known planet with liquid water and conditions supporting life.  
Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun, characterized by its reddish appearance due to iron-rich dust and features like the largest volcano, Olympus Mons.  
Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun, a gas giant with the largest storm (Great Red Spot) and no solid surface.  
Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun, renowned for its extensive ring system composed of ice and rock particles.  
Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun, an ice giant with a blue-green hue caused by methane in its atmosphere and a unique axial tilt.  
Neptune is the eighth and farthest planet from the Sun, a blue ice giant with the fastest winds in the Solar System and a turbulent atmosphere.
