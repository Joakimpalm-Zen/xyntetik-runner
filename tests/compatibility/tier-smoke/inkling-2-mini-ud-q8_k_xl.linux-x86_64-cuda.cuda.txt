Mercury is a small, rocky, cratered planet with extreme temperature swings.  
Venus is a hot, thick-atmosphere world with a crushing CO₂ blanket and no significant plate tectonics.  
Earth is the only known world with life, liquid surface water, and a breathable oxygen-rich atmosphere.  
Mars is a cold, reddish, thin-atmosphere planet with polar ice caps and towering volcanoes.  
Jupiter is the largest planet, a gas giant with a Great Red Spot and many moons.  
Saturn is a gas giant famous for its extensive ring system and low average density.  
Uranus is an ice giant with a strongly tilted axis and a pale blue-green appearance.  
Neptune is a deep-blue ice giant with powerful winds and a dynamic atmosphere.
