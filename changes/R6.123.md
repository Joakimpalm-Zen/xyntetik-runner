- **Metal computes exp with the precise function, so a build gives the same SiLU, softmax and gate values on every Apple GPU generation it was measured on** (R6.123).
  - **Limits first:** Metal output bits can change once on any Mac, where the fast and the precise exp of an input differ. Measured on two Apple GPU generations (an Apple M1 and an Apple M5 Max) only. Speed on the Apple M1 (SmolLM2-1.7B Q4_K_M, 8 interleaved pairs of `--bench-json`): decode 1.0034 [0.9988, 1.0098], no difference shown; prompt 0.9922 [0.9503, 1.0452], not resolved on a machine under memory pressure. RESULTS_M5.
  - **What was wrong:** the Metal library is compiled at run time with the compiler's default fast math, and its fast exp gives different results on an Apple M5 Max's GPU than on an Apple M1's. `test-kv-tol`'s k8v4 arm on `test-q40.gguf` (K q8_0, V fp4) read 5.48 times its floor on the Apple M5 Max (limit 3.0) and 0.01 times on the Apple M1, the same bytes in every release back to 1.1.3, so the released gate failed on that device class. A bisection on the Apple M5 Max named it: precise math functions for the whole library brought the arm to 0.01x; exact scale decoders did not; exp alone did, and of its call sites the SiLU in `k_silu_mul` alone did.
  - **The fix:** all 27 exp calls in `src/kernels.metal` (attention softmax, chunk combine, SiLU, sigmoid and SwiGLU gates, the erf approximation, MoE routing, the recurrent cores) call `precise::exp`, as the rope's sin and cos already do. The rest of the library keeps fast math: precise math functions for every function moved the Q4_K fixture's k8v4 arm on the Apple M5 Max from 0.03x to 1.29x, and safe math put its q8 arm at 10.72x.
  - **Measured:** on the Apple M5 Max, `test-q40.gguf` passes (k8v4 0.01x) and `test-q4k.gguf`'s k8v4 arm reads 0.04x. On the Apple M1, `test-q40.gguf`'s q8 arm moves from 0.19x to 0.02x and its k8v4 arm stays at 0.01x; `test-q4k.gguf` reads the same before and after (q8 0.51x, fp4 0.02x, k8v4 0.10x); the SmolLM2-1.7B Q4_K_M Metal pin still matches.
