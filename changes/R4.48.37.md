- **Inkling 2 Mini's `--info fit` says which weights stay on the host** (R4.48.37).
  - A tensor whose type has no CUDA product kernel (anything but F32, BF16, Q8_0, NVFP4 and IQ3_XXS) stays on the host,
    and its products run on the CPU. The fit still said "every weight on the device". On a published IQ3_M file, only
    0.2 GB of about 14 GB was on the device.
  - The fit now gives the device's share of the product weight bytes, and a host-weights line with the tensors left
    on the host and their types. The verdict says how many expert stacks run on the host, and the load's banner names
    the tensors that stay there.
  - A file whose product weights are all on the device keeps its lines as before.
