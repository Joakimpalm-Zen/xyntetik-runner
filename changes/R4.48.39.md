- **Inkling 2 Mini on CUDA runs community files' Q4_K tensors on the device** (R4.48.39).
  - A Q4_K tensor, dense or expert stack, used to stay on the host with its products on the CPU. It now runs on the
    device: the generic batch-1 kernel for its products, and a segmented twin of it for its expert stacks.
  - The class against the CPU route is the route's own tolerance class. Inside CUDA the expert body is byte-identical
    to per-expert launches of the batch-1 kernel.
  - Prompt reading on these stacks is one column at a time, so it is slower than on NVFP4 stacks, which run as
    batched tiles.
  - An expert stack now goes to the device only when the expert body runs its type.
