- **Inkling 2 Mini on CUDA runs community files' Q4_K, IQ4_NL, IQ3_S and Q5_0 tensors on the device** (R4.48.39).
  - A tensor of those types, dense or expert stack, used to stay on the host with its products on the CPU. It now
    runs on the device: the generic batch-1 kernel for its products, and a segmented twin of it for its expert stacks.
  - The class against the CPU route is the route's own tolerance class. Inside CUDA the expert body is byte-identical
    to per-expert launches of the batch-1 kernel.
  - Prompt reading on these stacks is one column at a time, so it is slower than on NVFP4 stacks, which run as
    batched tiles.
  - Q6_K and Q5_K run on the device as dense products. Their expert stacks stay on the host: an expert stack now goes
    to the device only when the expert body runs its type.
  - The CUDA fit charges the resident decode step only for a file whose stacks can take it (NVFP4 or BF16), by the
    step's own test. Before, a file of these types carried about 10 MB of a step it never set up. The 16 GB file's
    IQ3_XXS stacks cannot take the step either, so its fit now drops those bytes and equals its fit without the step.
