- **Tulu 3's chat template, rendered exactly** (R4.50.11).
  - **Limits first:**
    - One runner template per vocabulary, because the template ends an assistant turn with the file's own end
      token. `tulu3` writes Tulu 3's `<|end_of_text|>`, `tulu3-olmo2` OLMo 2's `<|endoftext|>`, and detection picks
      by the file's EOS. A file with this template and any other EOS keeps its former zephyr reading.
    - Its template has no tool protocol, so the OpenAI tool protocol is refused on it with a 400, as on `olmo2`:
      - `tools`, `tool_calls` and `tool` turns on `/v1/chat/completions`;
      - `tools` and `function_call` items on `/v1/responses`;
      - `tools`, `tool_use` and `tool_result` blocks on `/v1/messages`.
    - `--info tools` reports `"tool_family":"none"`.
    - AllenAI publishes no Tulu 3 GGUF. Its token-level check ran on the header of a community conversion
      (bartowski/Llama-3.1-Tulu-3-8B-GGUF, metadata only, no weights). That header matches the publisher's
      tokenizer.json at every id, in its merges and specials, and in its BOS and EOS. OLMo 2 32B's check ran on
      its publisher GGUF's header.
    - This is the template only. Tulu 3 is a Llama 3.1 fine-tune and runs on the Llama route. OLMo 2's
      architecture is not admitted yet (R4.50.6).
  - **What it is:**
    - Tulu 3's template is in allenai/Llama-3.1-Tulu-3 8B, 70B and 405B. allenai/OLMo-2-0325-32B-Instruct ships it
      byte for byte.
    - It is OLMo 2 Instruct's template without the BOS. Runner read it as zephyr before, which ends every turn with
      a literal `</s>`.
    - It now writes every branch of the publisher's template:
      - no BOS (the tokenizer's, where it adds one: Tulu 3's does, OLMo 2's does not);
      - a newline after a system or user turn;
      - the file's end token after an assistant turn, then a newline unless it is the last message;
      - the generation prompt after the last message.
    - `olmo2` shares the rendering after its BOS, and its cases are unchanged.
