- **Rewriting an Inkling 2 Mini file keeps its two-level NVFP4 exact, costs a looped block per application, and
  prunes only to one expert count** (R4.48.8.1).
  - Inkling 2 Mini's expert stacks are NVFP4 codes times a UE4M3 scale per 16 values, times a per-expert factor
    (`blk.N.ffn_*_exps.scale`) that the loader applies after the decode. A rewrite that keeps a stack's type copies
    the stack and its factors bit for bit.
  - A rewrite that re-quantizes a stack now folds each expert's factor into the rows in the loader's order,
    `(code * block_scale) * factor`, and does not write that `.scale` tensor. Before, it re-quantized the rows
    without the factor and kept the `.scale`. That file was also correct, since the loader applies a `.scale` to
    any stack type, but `--quant-error` costed rows the file did not hold.
  - `--quant-error` decodes through the same function. Before, it ignored the per-expert factors, so each stack's
    error was off by its factors squared (16 times on a factor of 0.25), and `runner derive` ranked tensors on
    those costs. The report now costs exactly what the rewrite writes.
  - A block inside the loop runs `loop.count` times per token, so `--quant-error` now multiplies its error by
    `loop.count`, read from the file's own keys. Its bytes stay those of the one stored copy. An imatrix already
    held the right weights: its entries are keyed by the stored tensor, so a looped block's means are over all
    its passes.
  - `--prune-experts` now slices `ffn_gate_up_exps.scale` with its stack (`ffn_down_exps.scale` already was) and
    rewrites `inkling2_loop.expert_count` on a uniform plan. Before, the unsliced factor made the file one the
    loader refused.
  - A plan that leaves the layers at different expert counts is refused before writing, naming two layers that
    differ. The Inkling loader reads one `expert_count` and holds every stack to it, so such a file could not load.
  - Tests:
    - `tests/test_inkling2_rewrite.py`, on the generated tiny file. Eight of its tests were red on main; the keep
      test is a guard, green on main too. With factors that are powers of two, a fold shows exactly in the written
      Q8_0 blocks: the codes are the same as the unit-factor file's, and each block's scale is that expert's
      factor times the unit-factor scale, after a prune too.
    - `tests/test_inkling2_requant.c` holds the rewrite's decode to the loader's reference route. 196,608 values
      match, on a file with varied scales and factors that are not powers of two. The other association,
      `code * (block_scale * factor)`, differs on 6,970 of them, so the check can tell the two orders apart.
    - `tests/test_quantize_oom.c` adds an `inkling2_loop` fold, with every allocation failed in turn, and the
      non-uniform refusal.
  - Known limits:
    - Pruning an Inkling file to a different count per layer needs per-layer expert counts in its engine, fit and
      CUDA route. That is a separate item.
    - The CUDA route's expert products take F32, BF16, Q8_0, NVFP4 and IQ3_XXS. A stack re-quantized to another
      type runs its products on the host.
    - An NVFP4 stack (4.5 bits) declines every 4.5-bit target (`q4_0`, `q4_k`, `iq4_nl`) as not smaller; `q3_k`
      and `mxfp4` are smaller.
    - Gemma 4's per-expert `ffn_down_exps.scale` is not folded here, and `--quant-error` does not weight by it. Its
      files are unchanged by this entry.
