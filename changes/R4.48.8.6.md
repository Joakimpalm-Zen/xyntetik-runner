- **Device-format writers (`IQ3_XXS` and two-level `NVFP4`) and `keep` top ladder rung for `derive`** (R4.48.8.6).
  - `src/quantize.c` now writes `iq3_xxs` (`T_IQ3_XXS`, 98 bytes per 256 weights, `MOSTLY_IQ3_XXS = 23`) with a
    faithful port of `llama.cpp`'s `quantize_row_iq3_xxs_impl(256, ...)` (unweighted and `--imatrix`-weighted).
    Its lookup tables are derived directly from `quants_iq3xxs_grid()` (`src/quants_iq_grids.h`) into static
    storage (~61.6 KiB, zero heap allocations). Verified byte-identical (`0` differing bytes across `3,136` bytes,
    both unweighted and imatrix-weighted) against pinned `ggml-quants.c`.
  - `src/quantize.c` now writes two-level `nvfp4` (`T_NVFP4`, 36 bytes per 64 weights, `MOSTLY_NVFP4 = 39`):
    per-expert F32 `.scale` (`amax / (6 * 448)`, `1.0` fallback for zero experts) for `inkling2_loop` expert
    stacks and per-tensor `[1]` F32 `.scale` otherwise, paired with 16-element UE4M3 block scales and E2M1 nibbles
    matching the loader's `(code * block_scale) * scale` association. Ours encodes all 127 finite UE4M3 codes
    (`0x00..0x7E`, including `[256..448]` and subnormal carry into `0x08`, never emitting `0x7F`) with IEEE
    round-to-nearest-even. Synthesized `.scale` companions are computed by a streaming O(row)-RAM pre-pass and
    written cleanly through header, layout, and data stages (including under uniform `--prune-experts`).
    `inkling2_loop` dense tensors decline `nvfp4` cleanly (matching `inkling2_gguf.c`'s loader contract).
  - `--quant-error` costs `iq3_xxs` (with `--imatrix` and `loop.count`) and two-level `nvfp4` (with the same
    streaming scale pre-pass, `(code * block_scale) * f` decode, synthesized `.scale` bytes, `0.0` error on
    already-`NVFP4` stacks, and `null` on `inkling2_loop` dense tensors), and emits `source_bytes` per tensor.
  - `runner derive --target-size --ladder` (and `scripts/type-plan-by-sensitivity.py`) now accept `keep` as the
    top rung (rung `0` only), clamping non-shrinking lower rungs to `(source_bytes, 0.0)` during planning so

    `--ladder keep,nvfp4,iq3_xxs` and `--ladder keep,q8_0,nvfp4,iq3_xxs`.
