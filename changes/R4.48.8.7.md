  - **R4.48.8.7 (Gemma 4 `ffn_down_exps.scale` costing):**
    - Changes no engine output: only `--quant-error` costs and `derive` plans for Gemma 4 MoE files.
    - In Gemma 4 MoE (`general.architecture == "gemma4"`), `blk.N.ffn_down_exps.scale` (`[n_expert]`) is a learned
      per-expert output multiplier applied after the down-projection matvec (`src/model.c:10717`) and kept
      untouched across rewrites (unlike Inkling 2 Mini's folded two-level NVFP4 `.scale`). Previously
      `--quant-error` weighted every Gemma 4 expert by `1.0` instead of `f[e]^2`, mis-ranking
      `blk.N.ffn_down_exps.weight` in `runner derive --target-size`.
    - `src/quantize.c` now decodes `blk.N.ffn_down_exps.scale` (F32, BF16, F16, or any supported type, matching
      `model.c`'s `tensor_to_f32`) in `gemma4_down_exps_scales()`, quantizes the unscaled stored row (matching
      what `--quantize` writes), and weights expert `e`'s row error by `f[e]^2` in `double` (with a guard against
      double-counting `t->scale` if `n_expert == 1`).
    - `nvfp4_allowed_for_tensor()` now declines `T_NVFP4` on any non-`inkling2_loop` tensor that already owns a
      non-scalar `.scale` companion (and on Gemma 4 `ffn_down_exps.weight` with `.scale`), so `--quant nvfp4`
      never overwrites Gemma 4's learned `[n_expert]` `.scale` with a `[1]` tensor.
    - Audit of committed Gemma 4 records and cards: no committed Gemma 4 MoE `derive` record or card exists in
      the repository that would plan differently now. All committed `derive` records (`docs/compat-reports/qwen3-*`,
      `r4.7.3-*`, `r4.32-*`, `mellum2.1-*`, `hemmingway-*`) target other families; the committed Gemma 4 reports
      (`docs/compat-reports/gemma-4-e2b-ple-quant-2026-10-31/`, `docs/gemma4-26b-reference-2026-11-10.md`,
      `docs/compat-reports/cpu-cuda-128/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it-*`) are dense E2B hand-written PLE isolation plans or
      engine reference checks, all unaffected.
    - Tests: `tests/test_gemma4_quant_error.py` (6 red-first tests, all 6 red on base, green here):
      uniform `f^2` scaling, per-expert asymmetric decomposition + negative and BF16/F16 `.scale` decoding, exact
      match to independently decoded rewritten `q4_0` bytes on `[0.85, 1.15]` with `.scale` kept bit-for-bit,

  - **R4.53 (imatrix float-invariance comment and test):**
    - Corrected the `src/quantize.c` imatrix comment and `tests/test_quantize.c`: in float32, identical quantized
      bytes hold only for power-of-two imatrix rescalings (exponent-only shift); non-power-of-two rescalings
      (such as `x7` or a non-power-of-two `in_sum2 / counts` ratio) can flip near-ties in the weighted
      candidate-scale float searches (shared with `ggml`).
    - Added `quantize_imatrix_entry()` (`src/quants.h`, `src/quantize.c`) so `tests/test_quantize.c` verifies
      value-level agreement between the `.dat` and GGUF readers up to the factor, asserts byte equality for
      power-of-two factors (`x4` and GGUF `4*imx / 4`) and equal output length (`l1 == l7`), and reports `x7`
      differing byte counts without asserting them (since whether a near-tie flips depends on float summation
      order and FMA contraction). Measured on this `256 x 256` fixture at this commit: `x4` gives `0` differing
      bytes; `x7` flips `q4_0=3`, `q4_k=74`, `iq4_nl=7`, `iq3_xxs=3` bytes.
    - Repo audit: no other doc, test, or committed record relies on arbitrary-factor imatrix byte-identity.
