- **`runner derive` and `runner --score` now support RAM-bounded pre-computed reference logits (`--reference logits:MANIFEST.json` and `--score-ref MANIFEST.json`)** (R4.48.8.3).
  - Adds a pure-C streaming reference logits reader and exact full-vocabulary float64 KL scorer (`src/ref_logits.h`, `src/ref_logits.c`) reusing `src/npy.h` and `src/envelope.h`. Scoring streams one float32 logit row at a time (`O(V)` RAM, under 2 MB extra for Inkling 2 Mini) with no Python or torch dependency during `derive`, and computes the exact float64 `log_softmax` KL formula of `compare_full.py`.
  - Accepts pinned `xyntetik.runner.ref-logits.v1` manifests referencing either NumPy `.npy` or headerless `raw-f32` (`*.logits.f32`) float32 `[N, V]` files (dense or sparse via `row_indices`), enforcing path containment, non-symlink regular-file checks, live SHA-256 verification before `--rewrite`, optional corpus SHA-256 verification, window coverage, vocabulary/token/`n_special` parity, and fail-closed non-finite logit rejection.
  - Writes `OUT.gold.json` in `xyntetik.runner.gold-logits.v4` schema so bootstrap CIs, bar verdicts, `--compare`, and `--card-from` work identically, and records manifest/logits SHA-256 digests and `producer_mode` in `OUT.derive.json`. Also adds `--ram-cap` preflight checking in `derive`.
  - Adds `scripts/make-ref-logits.py` with `wrap` (O(1) RAM wrapping of existing pinned `*.logits.f32` / `.npy` dumps), `plan` (explicit `--ram-cap-gib` budgeting without silently falling back to an unvalidated mode), `produce` (`--mode patched` default, `--mode patched-stream` explicit opt-in), and `control` (bit-for-bit parity verification of `patched` vs `patched-stream` on a short read).
