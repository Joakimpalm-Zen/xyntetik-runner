- **Inkling 2 Mini's 8-bit K/V cache now runs on the CUDA route too** (R4.48.8.5.1).
  - `--kv q8` under `RUNNER_INKLING2_CUDA=1` stores q8 rings on the device. Before this it kept f32 with a note.
    The TEST-ONLY `RUNNER_INKLING2_CUDA_TEST_KVQ8` that let the device gates through is removed from the code,
    the tests and the docs.
  - The gates that cleared it, all before this change:
    - (n8): the device's q8 cores give the float32 kernels' bits over the round-tripped rows (0 and 0 differing
      values);
    - (n9): the device store writes the host's `q8_quant_row` bytes (0 of 12 rows differ);
    - (c): teacher-forced against the CPU's q8 cache, in `test_kv_tol.c`'s tolerance class.
      - The tiny file: 8.383e-07 against 3 x 1.059e-06.
      - The real file: 3.673e-02 against its bar of 1.161e-01, with 0 decisive top-1 flips.
    - (d): the fidelity cost of q8 against f32 on the real file is flat with depth. The worst band means are
      2.651e-03, 2.270e-03 and 1.927e-03 on the three books, and margin-qualified top-1 is at least 0.9996 in every
      band.
  - `tests/test_inkling2_kv.py::test_q8_engages_on_the_device`, on a CUDA host, requires the load to say `K/V q8`
    with no note keeping f32. It fails without this change.
  - Known limits:
    - Gate (c) holds the device to the CPU within a tolerance; token identity is never claimed.
    - The device tests run on a CUDA host only, so CI's runners skip them.
