- **Inkling 2 Mini's resident CUDA decode step takes IQ3_XXS expert stacks** (R4.48.35).
  - The 16 GB file (IQ3_XXS gate_up, NVFP4 down) used to decode on the non-resident path. The step runs its expert
    products as bounded launches, and those had no IQ3_XXS kernel.
  - A bounded twin of the IQ3_XXS segmented kernel now serves them. `k_mvseg_iq3_xxs_n` is the sized kernel's body
    verbatim, behind the two lines every bounded twin starts with.
  - The step admits an expert stack only when the module has its bounded kernel, so an older module declines the step
    instead of failing it.
  - The step's output stays byte-identical to the non-resident route. `tests/test_inkling2_cuda.c` holds the bounded
    expert body to the sized one on the 16 GB file's mix with boundary blocks, and the whole step to the non-resident
    route on a new IQ3_XXS-expert fixture.
