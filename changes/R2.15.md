- **Apertus: thinking is separated from the answer** (R2.15). With `enable_thinking` on, an Apertus model's whole deliberation came back as the answer, unmarked, and `reasoning_content` was empty. The model marks its deliberation with two control tokens, which decode to no text, and Runner finds a reasoning block by reading text. A chat turn now reads those two tokens by name: `<|inner_prefix|>` and `<|inner_suffix|>` in Apertus v1.5's vocabulary, `<think>` and `</think>` in the 2509 files.
  - **What a client sees:** `reasoning_content` on `/v1/chat/completions` (streamed and not), a reasoning item on `/v1/responses`, a thinking block on `/v1/messages`, and the answer alone as the content. `/v1/messages` refused `thinking: enabled` for this family before.
  - **Unchanged:** a turn without thinking; a raw `/v1/completions`, whose text is the tokens as they decode.
  - **Not covered:** thinking together with a JSON schema, JSON mode or constrained tool calls on this family is as it was: the constraint starts at the first token, so the model does not deliberate there.
