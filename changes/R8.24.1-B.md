- **`--train-gpu` runs a dense gated SiLU FFN on the device whole: on an RTX 5070 host a step is about 1.56x faster than main d9b54174's (1.16x over list A); a default build's adapter bytes change once** (R8.24.1, list B).
  - The FFN of Qwen3, Llama and their kin (a dense gated SiLU, no post-FFN norm or per-layer embeddings) runs on the device in the taped forward, the backward's recompute and its three sites: the products, the adapter's products at gate, up and down, and the activation and its adjoint. Its activations and gradients stay on the device between them; the FFN sites' adapters and gradients go up and come back once a backward, so the optimizer, the gradient norms and checkpoints are as before. It runs when the device holds every tensor, and the run says so once on stderr, or why not; `RUNNER_TRAIN_DEVICE_STEP=0` keeps the host's FFN for comparison.
  - The new kernels are host-chain twins (`kernels_train.cuh`): each element computed as a strict build's host computes it (`make T3=1`: the host's order of operations, nothing contracted, the portable exp of `src/pmath.h`). A T3 build's `--train-gpu` adapter is therefore the one it was before, byte for byte (shown on Qwen3-4B Q4_K_M, 5 steps). A default build's adapter changes once, because its host had used the C library's `expf` and `-ffast-math` there; after the change the default and T3 builds compute that part of the step alike, so it depends on the GPU and Runner's kernels, not on the host's compiler or C library. The CPU trainer's bytes do not change.
  - Measured on that host (Qwen3-4B Q4_K_M, the ToolUse training record's data, rank 8 on every projection, ctx 128, 20 steps, 8 threads; the median of steps 2 to 20, interleaved runs): 1.12 s a step, against 1.30 with list A and 1.75 for main d9b54174.
  - Limits: the documented tolerance against a CPU run (1e-4 over 4 steps) is shown on the test fixtures. On a real model a training run is a trajectory, and any two arithmetic variants drift apart at the 1e-3 level within a few steps; on the recipe above, each step's loss against a T3 CPU run's: step 1 (the forward alone) 1.3e-4, steps 2 to 5 6.0e-4, 1.8e-5, 9.5e-4, 6.1e-4, inside the spread of main's own four builds ([docs/lora.md](docs/lora.md#training-on-the-gpu---train-gpu) has the table).
  - Not yet on the device: attention, the norms, rope, the head, the other projections' adapter products and the optimizer.
