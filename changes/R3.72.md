- **A turn on the Apple GPU's tiled decode microbatch records its decode schedule, and `--verify` replays it** (R3.72; `RUNNER_METAL_BATCH_TILES=1`, opt-in).
  - The receipt now records which decode positions ran the GEMM tiles and at what microbatch width (`profile.route.micro_steps`, runs of `[first position, count, width]`), beside `micro: "tiles"`. A position the list does not hold was decoded on the identity route.
  - `--verify` replays such a record on a Metal build. Each listed position decodes as column 0 of a microbatch of its recorded width beside stand-ins (the prompt's first token at position 0); the other positions decode solo. The verifier then checks that the tiles ran at exactly the recorded steps.
  - It rests on a measured fact (SmolLM2-1.7B Q8_0): under the tiles a column's result is the same at widths 6 to 8, in columns 0, 3 and 7, and whatever the other columns hold.
  - Refused by name, never replayed solo: no schedule (a record from before this), a truncated or malformed one (a tiled step under 6 columns included), a build without Metal or a record naming another backend, and a verifier whose route differs from the record's.
  - Cost: one microbatch step per listed position plus the stand-ins' load. On SmolLM2-1.7B Q8_0, a 256-position score with the same stand-ins, every position tiled, took 33.9 s against 12.4 s solo.
