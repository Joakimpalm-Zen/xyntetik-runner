- **Inkling 2 Mini gets an 8-bit K/V cache on its CPU and CUDA routes, and a fit that counts what its load allocates**
  (R4.48.8.5).
  - `--kv q8` stores each K and V row as the house's q8_0 row (quants.c `q8_quant_row`), on the full applications
    and the sliding buffers alike. The attention cores read value e as `(float)q * d`, which is exact in float32, so
    each route's arithmetic is unchanged.
    - The reference route dequantizes a head row for K and reads V element by element.
    - The fast route uses the same head row for K through its dot, and accumulates V in the float32 path's own
      lanes and fmas (NEON and AVX2).
  - Gate (a), `tests/test_inkling2_kvq8.c`: the q8 cache's logits equal, bit for bit, a float32 cache holding each
    row's q8 round trip. It holds on both CPU routes, at `-b 5` and on the `-b 1` decode path, over 40 positions
    and an 8-position window, so the buffers slide. A mutant that reads the wrong half of each block fails it.
  - Gate (b), `tests/test_inkling2_kv.py`: q8 engages, and keeps every bit invariance the float32 cache holds
    (thread counts, prompt chunk sizes), pair by pair.
  - `--kv f16`, `k8v4` and `fp4` keep f32 with a note, and so does a head_dim that is not a multiple of 32.
    `--kv-ring off` notes that the sliding applications always keep a window ring.
  - One set of sizes serves the cache, the load line and `--info fit`, so they cannot disagree.
    - The fit now reads `--kv` and `--parallel`, and prints exact bytes.
    - It counts the sliding applications from the file's own pattern; it printed 0 for the tiny file's 4, from a
      hard-coded 8 full applications.
    - It no longer says "CPU only (no GPU path)".
  - On the CUDA route (`RUNNER_INKLING2_CUDA=1`), `--info fit` places the file as its load would: a dry run of
    the load's placement that binds the device, reserves the buffers, reads the free memory and uploads nothing.
    - It prints the weights, the buffers, the small tensors and the step's, every slot's K/V rings and histories,
      the 512 MiB kept, a `non-weight` line in exact bytes, and FITS, FITS WITH --kv q8 or DOES NOT FIT.
  - The device half is written:
    - the q8 twins of the attention kernels, with only their K and V loads changed, so the float32 kernels' source
      and PTX are as they were;
    - a store kernel with the KV store's q8 arithmetic, used by the block path and the resident step;
    - the host's own `q8_quant_row` on the host-computed path;
    - each slot's sliding rings packed into one allocation per side.
  - Bytes at 64K on Inkling 2 Mini, one slot, `-b 256`:
    - Host cache: f32 4,719,378,432 (4.40 GiB), q8 1,257,483,264 (1.17 GiB).
    - Device rings, histories and post copies: f32 4,719,771,648, q8 1,259,470,848 (1.17 GiB), pinned by
      `tests/test_inkling2_kvq8.c`.
    - Packing alone saves 52 MiB of the q8 figure (160 MiB of sliding rings become 108).
  - Known limits:
    - The device's q8 rings are held to the CPU's q8 cache, teacher-forced, in `test_kv_tol.c`'s tolerance class:
      never token identity.
      - The gates are (n8), (n9) and gate (c) in `tests/test_inkling2_cuda.c`, and
        `tests/test_inkling2_kv.py::test_q8_engages_on_the_device`.
      - The PTX was regenerated on a CUDA host, in its own commit.
    - The fidelity cost of q8 against f32 on the real file is measured there, by the KLD scorer, not assumed here.
    - The fast route dequantizes a K head once per query head (three times for each K/V head on the real file).
      Its speed at long context is unmeasured.
