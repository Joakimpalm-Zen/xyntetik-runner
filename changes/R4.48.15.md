This change so far holds the tests the route must pass, written before it: `tests/test_inkling2_fast.c` (the route switch, closeness to the reference route without equality, thread and batch-width invariance, no float32 weight copies, and an 8-bit activation control that must fall further from the reference), `tests/test_nvfp4_dot.c` (a SIMD NVFP4 dot, its decode bit for bit, its accuracy, its multi-column twin) and `tests/test_inkling2_fast.py` (`--info fit` naming the route).
