- **Inkling 2 Mini: a fast CPU route** (R4.48.15, work in progress).
  - **Limits first:** not yet a route. This change so far holds the tests the route must pass, written before it: `tests/test_inkling2_fast.c` (the route switch, closeness to the reference route without equality, thread and batch-width invariance, no float32 weight copies, and an 8-bit activation control that must fall further from the reference), `tests/test_nvfp4_dot.c` (a SIMD NVFP4 dot, its decode bit for bit, its accuracy, its multi-column twin) and `tests/test_inkling2_fast.py` (`--info fit` naming the route). They fail until the route lands.
  - `tests/inkling2_tiny.py` writes the tiny inkling2_loop file with its dense projections F32, BF16 or Q8_0, so every weight type a real file carries reaches the engine; the loader's hardening test takes it from there.
  - **NVFP4 gets SIMD kernels:** the dot and the prefill tile's decode on AVX2 + FMA and on NEON (the scalar loop stays for other builds). The dot keeps float32 activations, takes the E2M1 codes through a byte shuffle and each sub-block's UE4M3 scale from a table, and sums the even and the odd sub-blocks in two accumulators; it is within 1e-7 of a double-precision dot. The decode gives the scalar decode's bits for every code at every scale.
